7. Data quality rules (each one found in the data, not assumed)

RuleHow it surfaced
Swiss stop = UIC prefix 85 (string), not integer division19% 'Swiss' stops instead of 98% (7- vs 9-digit BPUIC)
|delay| > 1 day is a broken timestamp, not a delay8,821 records, down to −56 years; mean shifts, p95 does not
One canonical raw schema across format evolutionv2 had no SLOID before 2025-10; readers refused mixed files
Product label normalization'Bua' as an 8th product on 5,250 rows
Prefix 85 means 'in the Swiss register', not 'in Switzerland'Italian stations (Luino line) among the worst stops
Line identity by route fingerprint, not vendor name12 renames found (≈1% of volume drift); 99% was new coverage

8. Methodology

PrincipleWhy
Quantiles, not means8k garbage values moved the mean, not a single p95.
Design effectArrivals within a day are correlated; iid bootstrap CIs were 2.5–10× too narrow. We resample whole days (GEMM on GPU).
Like-for-like + FDR45% of line-level 'changes' were route rebuilds, volume shifts or noise. Panel of stable lines, Benjamini–Hochberg 5%.
Reporting policyp95 shown only with n ≥ 200 (≥ 10 obs above p95); 'precise' only if CI half-width ≤ max(60 s, 20%).
